Turmeric (Haldi)

Sanskrit name: Haridra
Botanical name: Curcuma longa

Properties (as per Ayurveda):
- Rasa (taste): Tikta (bitter), Katu (pungent)
- Virya (potency): Ushna (hot)
- Effect on Doshas: Balances Kapha and Vata, may increase Pitta in excess

Common uses:
- Wound healing: A paste of turmeric powder and water can be applied to minor cuts as a traditional antiseptic.
- Cough and cold: Turmeric milk (Haldi Doodh / Golden Milk) is a classic home remedy for cough, cold, and body ache.
- Joint pain: Turmeric with warm milk or turmeric with warm water is traditionally used to reduce mild joint inflammation.
- Immunity: Regular small doses of turmeric in food are believed to support general immunity.

Simple home preparation (Golden Milk):
1. Take 1 cup of warm milk.
2. Add half a teaspoon of turmeric powder and a pinch of black pepper (improves absorption).
3. Add honey to taste once lukewarm.
4. Drink once daily, preferably at night.

Caution: High doses may cause stomach upset. People on blood-thinning medication should consult a doctor before regular use.
