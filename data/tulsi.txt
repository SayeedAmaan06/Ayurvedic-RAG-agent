Tulsi (Holy Basil)

Sanskrit name: Tulsi
Botanical name: Ocimum sanctum

Properties (as per Ayurveda):
- Rasa (taste): Katu (pungent), Tikta (bitter)
- Virya (potency): Ushna (hot)
- Effect on Doshas: Balances Kapha and Vata, may increase Pitta in excess

Common uses:
- Cough, cold, and congestion: Chew 4-5 fresh tulsi leaves or boil them in water with ginger and honey to make a decoction (kadha).
- Fever: Tulsi kadha with black pepper and ginger is a traditional home remedy for common fever.
- Stress and immunity: Tulsi is considered an adaptogen; regular tulsi tea is used to support immunity and reduce stress.
- Sore throat: Gargle with warm water boiled with tulsi leaves and a pinch of salt.

Simple home preparation (Tulsi Kadha):
1. Take 8-10 fresh tulsi leaves.
2. Boil in 2 cups of water with a small piece of ginger.
3. Reduce to 1 cup, strain, add honey once lukewarm.
4. Drink twice a day during cold/cough.

Caution: Avoid excessive long-term use during pregnancy without consulting a doctor. Not a replacement for medical treatment in serious infections.
