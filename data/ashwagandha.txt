Ashwagandha

Sanskrit name: Ashwagandha
Botanical name: Withania somnifera

Properties (as per Ayurveda):
- Rasa (taste): Katu (pungent), Tikta (bitter), Kashaya (astringent)
- Virya (potency): Ushna (hot)
- Effect on Doshas: Balances Vata and Kapha

Common uses:
- Stress and anxiety: Known as an adaptogen, traditionally used to help the body cope with stress.
- Sleep quality: Ashwagandha milk (warm milk with ashwagandha powder) is a traditional remedy taken at night to support sleep.
- Strength and stamina: Traditionally used to support muscle strength and general vitality, especially in the elderly.
- Low energy/fatigue: Used as a general rasayana (rejuvenative) tonic.

Simple home preparation (Ashwagandha Milk):
1. Take half a teaspoon of ashwagandha powder.
2. Mix into 1 cup of warm milk (or plant milk).
3. Add a pinch of nutmeg powder if desired.
4. Drink at night before sleep.

Caution: Not recommended during pregnancy. May interact with sedatives and thyroid medication - consult a doctor if on medication.
